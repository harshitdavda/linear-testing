# Pricing rules

- Discount codes: `WELCOME10` (10% off), `HALFOFF` (50% off). Codes are case-insensitive.
- Tax is applied after the discount.
- All amounts are integer cents and rounded to the nearest cent.
- Tax uses banker's rounding (round half to even): 2.5 cents rounds to 2, 3.5 cents rounds to 4.
