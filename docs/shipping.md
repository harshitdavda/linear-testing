# Shipping

Each country belongs to one zone: `domestic-us`, `eu` or `asia`.

| Zone | Method | First kilo | Each extra kilo | Free above | Days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| domestic-us | standard | $4.99 | $1.00 | $50.00 | 3–5 |
| domestic-us | express | $14.99 | $2.50 | — | 1–2 |
| eu | standard | €8.99 | €1.50 | €75.00 | 4–7 |
| eu | express | €24.99 | €4.00 | — | 2–3 |
| asia | standard | 12.99 | 2.00 | — | 6–10 |
| asia | express | 34.99 | 5.00 | — | 3–4 |

- Weight is rounded up to the next whole kilo. Anything up to 1 kg pays the first-kilo price.
- The free-shipping threshold is checked against the goods total **after** promotions.
- An empty order never pays shipping.
